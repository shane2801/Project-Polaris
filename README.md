## Overview

Project Polaris is not a traditional portfolio.

It is an interactive digital experience that documents the evolution of an engineer through software projects, architecture, machine learning, AI, simulations, and experimentation.

Instead of presenting projects as isolated achievements, Polaris connects them into a living constellation that shows how one idea led to another.

The website is designed around one simple principle:

> **Build once. Tell the story everywhere.**

---

## Vision

Most portfolios answer:

> *"What have you built?"*

Polaris answers:

> *"How did you become the engineer you are today?"*

Every project becomes a chapter.

Every chapter becomes part of a larger story.
